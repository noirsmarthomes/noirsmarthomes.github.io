Climate and essentials

Comfort and protection that run quietly in the background: temperature, water, and the network that keeps everything connected.

Climate and essentials · 01

Multi-zone climate control

Automated thermostats optimized for comfort.

What it means

Different areas of the home are controlled separately, so bedrooms can be cool at night while living areas stay comfortable during the day. Schedules and scenes adjust the temperature without you having to think about it.

What you get

  • Comfort set room by room, instead of one setting for the whole house
  • Eases down automatically when you are away
  • Adjust from your phone or a wall keypad, wherever you are
Climate and essentials · 02

Smart water and leak defense

Automatic shutoff valves, moisture tracking.

What it means

Moisture sensors in places like kitchens, bathrooms and utility rooms detect a leak early. A motorized valve can shut off the water automatically and alert your phone, before a small leak becomes major damage.

What you get

  • Early warning of leaks, even when you are away
  • Automatic shutoff so the water stops flowing
  • Peace of mind when the property is empty
Climate and essentials · 03

Enterprise networking

Concealed, high-speed backbone for uninterrupted command.

What it means

Every smart system depends on a dependable network. We design a wired backbone and well-placed access points, hidden from view, so everything from video to door locks stays connected.

What you get

  • Strong Wi-Fi across the whole property, including outdoors
  • Wired connections where reliability matters most
  • A network built to carry cameras, audio and controls together without slowing down
