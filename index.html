Questions

Good to know

How do I get started?

Book a private consultation. We look at your space and goals, then send a clear proposal.

Can you add smart systems to an existing home?

Yes. New builds are easiest because wiring can be hidden in the walls, but existing homes can be upgraded with a mix of wired and wireless solutions.

What happens if the internet goes down?

A well-designed system keeps core functions such as lights, locks and scenes working locally. We explain what stays available for each system.

Is it difficult to use?

No. Control is designed to feel simple: wall keypads, touchpanels, a phone app, or automated scenes that run on their own.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of the home and the systems you choose. The consultation gives you a clear scope and price.
