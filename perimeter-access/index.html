Perimeter and access

Your first layer of protection and your first impression. Perimeter and access systems control who comes in, how they arrive, and what you can see from anywhere.

Ask about thisBook a consultation
Perimeter and access · 01

Automated gates and doors

Proximity-based entry, seamless integration.

What it means

Gates, garage doors and entrance doors that open, close and lock on their own. Instead of keys or remotes, the system recognizes an authorized phone, tag or vehicle as it approaches and opens in time for you to drive straight through.

What you get

  • Arrive and enter without stopping or reaching for a key
  • Let visitors in from your phone after you see who is there
  • Works with your intercom, cameras and lighting, so the driveway lights up as the gate opens
Perimeter and access · 02

Smart locks and biometrics

Keyless, high-security access tailored to you.

What it means

Doors that unlock with a fingerprint, a code, a card or your phone. Each person in your household and each regular visitor gets their own credential, so you decide who can enter and when.

What you get

  • Lost a phone or card? Cancel that one credential instantly, with no need to change the locks
  • See a record of who entered and when
  • Doors lock themselves and join your scenes, so "Away" secures the whole home at once
Perimeter and access · 03

Automated pool covers

Smart motorized protection, scheduled or on-demand.

What it means

A motorized cover that opens and closes at the press of a button, on a schedule, or from your phone. It keeps the pool clean and secured whenever nobody is using it.

What you get

  • Less debris and evaporation while the pool is covered
  • An added layer of safety around the water
  • Opens on a schedule, or as part of an evening entertaining scene
Perimeter and access · 04

Perimeter surveillance and intrusion detection

High-definition tracking, motion sensors, alarms, and intercoms.

What it means

High-definition cameras, motion sensors, alarms and door intercoms working together as one system. If something moves where it should not, you are told and can see it straight away.

What you get

  • Live and recorded video on your phone, from anywhere
  • Alerts when motion or an intrusion is detected, with lights that react
  • Speak to visitors at the door without opening it
